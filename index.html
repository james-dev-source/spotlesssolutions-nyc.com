Frequently Asked Questions

What types of commercial spaces do you service? ▼
We specialize in commercial spaces such as offices, kitchens, bars, lounges, retail locations, and other commercial environments. We also provide residential cleaning for houses and apartments. If your space requires professional, reliable cleaning, we can customize a solution for you.
How does pricing work for commercial cleaning? ▼
Pricing is based on your facility size, cleaning frequency, scope of work, and disinfection needs. We focus on long-term partnerships and structured contracts rather than “cheap quick cleans,” with packages built to support premium, consistent results using professional-grade, eco-friendly products. Request a quote and we’ll provide a tailored proposal for your site.
What’s included in your commercial deep cleaning & disinfection service? ▼
Our deep cleaning and disinfection typically include detailed dusting, vacuuming and mopping, restroom sanitization, break room/kitchen cleaning, trash removal, high-touch surface disinfection (handles, switches, railings, shared equipment), and lobby/common area care. The exact scope is customized to your location and compliance requirements.
How often can you service our business? ▼
We customize schedules based on your operations: once a week, multiple times per week, or structured deep-clean rotations. Options include sending a larger team for intensive visits or a smaller team for recurring maintenance cleans. We’ll recommend a schedule that keeps your space consistently clean and presentable.
Do you provide the cleaning products and equipment? ▼
Yes. We provide all professional-grade, eco-friendly cleaning products and equipment needed for the job. Our disinfecting solutions are selected to be effective while safe for staff, guests, and sensitive environments where applicable.
What areas do you serve? ▼
We currently provide commercial and residential cleaning in Manhattan, Queens, Brooklyn, the Bronx, and New Jersey. Contact us at 917-983-0177 to confirm service availability for your location.
Do you offer residential cleaning? ▼
Yes! We provide full residential cleaning services for houses and apartments — including regular maintenance cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out, and recurring schedules. Residential cleaning is a major part of what we do, alongside our commercial services. Contact us or request a quote to get started.
How do I request a quote or get started? ▼
Click "Get a Free Quote" or "Schedule Cleaning" on this page, share your details, preferred schedule, and scope, and our team will follow up with a custom proposal. You can also call us at 917-983-0177 or email info@spotlesssolutionsnyc.com with your requirements.
